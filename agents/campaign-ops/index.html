Agent 01Live on the Google Ads API

It found a Fair Housing problem inside Google Ads, then waited for a person to say yes.

Campaign Ops is an AI agent for Google Ads. It plugs into Claude, proposes every change, and touches nothing until a person approves. This page is the record of its first run on a Google Ads test account, through Google's official API.

What was real, and what was not

Google's test accounts behave like normal accounts, except they never show ads. That shapes what this run can prove.

Real

  • The Google Ads accounts, campaigns, budgets, keywords and ads
  • The robot login, every change and the read back
  • The approval and the audit log

Simulated

  • Clicks, cost, leads and search terms
  • Test accounts never serve ads, so Google has none to report. Campaign Ops fills them in from its sample data

Never happened

  • Spending money. Test accounts cannot spend
  • Touching a live account. It refuses anything Google does not confirm is a test account

What it is not allowed to do

The rules live in Campaign Ops, not in the AI. Claude can read them, but it cannot change them.

Change anything without a person's approvalAn unused approval lapses after 60 minutes
Move a budget more than 30% up or 50% down at onceBigger moves have to happen in steps
Go past $250 a day per campaign, or $400 for the accountAnd never below $5 a day
Publish ad copy that fails the Fair Housing screenEvery ad it writes is screened first
Exclude searches about disability, families, vouchers or religionThose exclusions can shut out protected groups
Block your own brand, or a fifth of a campaign's conversionsNegative keywords are checked against real search terms
Make more than 10 changes an hourOr touch the same item twice within 30 minutes
Touch an account Google does not confirm is a test accountChecked before every single change, for now

One switch stops everything: Pause all changes

How the pieces fit

Campaign Ops is an MCP server: a connector that gives an AI app a fixed set of tools, and nothing more.
